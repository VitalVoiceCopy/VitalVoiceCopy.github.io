Where Klaviyo revenue usually goes missing

These are the first four things we check in every audit. They're the most common gaps, and the quickest to fix.

A welcome flow that's just a discount code

New subscribers get 10% off and nothing else, so the ones who don't buy that day forget you.

What we changeA sequence that sells your brand and points each subscriber to the right first product, so the discount isn't doing all the work.

Abandoned checkout that stops after one email

One reminder, then silence, even though most people who leave had a specific reason.

What we changeA short sequence that answers the usual reasons people leave: price, doubt and timing. Each email handles one.

Every campaign sent to the whole list

Sending to people who stopped opening months ago drags down deliverability for everyone else.

What we changeEngagement-based segments, so your best customers hear from you more and inactive contacts stop hurting your inbox placement.

Nothing after the first order

The customer you just paid to acquire hears nothing until the next sale email.

What we changePost-purchase and replenishment flows timed to how long your product actually lasts, so the second order isn't left to chance.
